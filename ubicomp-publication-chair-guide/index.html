Scope

This guide documents the UbiComp / ISWC publication-chair workflow. It may be useful to other ACM conferences, but their chairs should verify the current platform, ACM or vendor process, submission types, page limits, deadlines, and author instructions before adopting it.

02

Before the Call for Workshops

Freeze the rules

  • PlatformGeneral Chairs and Publication Chairs decide in writing whether camera-ready processing will use TAPS or Sheridan.
  • Workshop publication policyRecommended default: one archival type—Workshop Short Paper, maximum 6 pages plus unlimited pages for references.
  • Posters and demosNot suggested unless approved before the Call for Workshops.
  • CalendarUse common workshop submission, acceptance-notification, final-submission and camera-ready dates whenever possible.
  • WebsiteEnsure the website, calls, PCS instructions, acceptance templates and FAQ all name the same platform and policy.
  • OutputA written platform decision and a track-policy matrix listing submission type, page limit, reference policy and deadlines.

04

Immediately After the Main-Paper Acceptance Notification

Collect main-paper XML

  • Owner to contactPC Chairs.
  • StartSend the XML request on the main-paper acceptance-notification date.
  • Suggested deadlineWithin three business days after the main-paper acceptance notification.
  • Required contentAll accepted papers, complete titles, IDs, authors, contact authors, submission types and withdrawals.
05

Immediately After the Workshop-Paper Acceptance Notification

Collect workshop XML

  • Owner to contactWorkshop Chairs, who contact every Workshop Organizer.
  • StartSend the request on the common workshop acceptance-notification date.
  • Suggested deadlineWithin three business days after the workshop-paper acceptance notification.
  • Required packageOne clearly named XML file per workshop, plus its accepted-paper count, paper type, page limit and withdrawal list.
06

Before Handoff

Validate every XML

  • Parse checkValid XML with at least one <paper> record when papers are expected.
  • Count checkXML count matches the accepted-paper count reported by the chairs.
  • Identity checkMatch Track + Manuscript ID + Full Paper Title. Never rely on ID alone.
  • Policy checkVerify submission type, page limit, reference policy and withdrawals.
  • Empty XMLConfirm the PCS track is in Final Submission and export it again.
07

Selected Platform

Hand off the validated package

TAPS

Upload through ACM

Use the link provided by Adrienne, then confirm the system begins sending eRights and camera-ready communications.

Sheridan

Send the package to Lisa

Include track-policy information, request the processed-paper list, and confirm authors receive the required links and instructions.

08

Until the Camera-Ready Deadline

Reconcile and resolve exceptions

  • CompareReconcile every XML against the TAPS/Sheridan processed-paper list.
  • ClassifyMark each track Complete, Partial, Misclassified, Not Processed, Empty XML or Withdrawn.
  • Report correctionsAlways provide Track, Manuscript ID, full title, expected type, current type and requested action.
  • Author readinessConfirm that all accepted authors received eRights and the correct camera-ready instructions.
  • Internal deadlineResolve the exception list at least five business days before the camera-ready deadline.
09

After the Camera-Ready Deadline

Close out and leave a clean archive

  • Final auditConfirm no accepted paper is missing or incorrectly classified and that withdrawals are excluded.
  • ArchiveSave XML files, policy decisions, processed-paper lists, correction emails, contacts and final counts for the next Publication Chairs.
  • Production QATrack unresolved DOI, ISBN, metadata or author-name issues through final production.
